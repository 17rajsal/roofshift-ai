# RoofShift AI — Data Sources, Methodologies & Provenance

This document details the scientific references, institutional benchmarks, and solar data sources utilized across RoofShift AI's modeling pipelines.

---

## 1. Solar Irradiance & Climatology Data

### Primary Source: NASA POWER API
- **Full Title:** NASA Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER)
- **Dataset:** Climatological Point API (30-year historical satellite and meteorological reanalysis)
- **Endpoint:** `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/climatology/point`
- **Parameter Used:** `ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN` (All Sky Surface Shortwave Downward Irradiance / Global Horizontal Irradiance, in $\text{kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$).
- **Spatial Resolution:** $0.5^\circ \times 0.5^\circ$ latitude/longitude grid cells.
- **Caching Strategy:** RoofShift AI caches retrieved monthly GHI coordinates rounded to $0.1^\circ$ (~11 km radius) in the `solar_climatology_cache` table to prevent redundant external API calls and ensure zero latency during repeat runs.
- **Fallback Provenance:** If the NASA API times out, the system automatically falls back to Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) / National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) regional solar radiation benchmarks for North India ($4.80\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$ annual average).

## 2. Geospatial Mapping & Geocoding

### Primary Source: OpenStreetMap & Nominatim
- **Service:** Nominatim Open-Source Geocoding API
- **Endpoint:** `https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search`
- **Usage:** Resolves Indian campus names, educational institutes, and municipal cities to WGS-84 coordinates.
- **Map Visuals:** Standard OpenStreetMap tile layers rendered via Leaflet.js with geodesic polygon surface area calculations.
- **Cloud Adapter:** Supports Amazon Location Service place index (`RoofShiftPlaceIndex`) as an active cloud adapter.

## 3. Financial & Tariff Modeling

### Regulatory Benchmark: Indian State Electricity Regulatory Commissions (SERCs)
- **Model Basis:** Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERC) and typical commercial/institutional tariffs in India.
- **Default Grid Import Tariff:** **₹8.50 / kWh** (reflective of non-domestic/institutional high-tension supply brackets).
- **Default Export Net-Metering Tariff:** **₹3.50 / kWh** (reflecting typical APPC / average power purchase cost feed-in rates credited by distribution companies).
- **Turnkey Capital Expenditure (Capex):** ₹50,000 / kWp installed (including Tier-1 monocrystalline PERC modules, on-grid inverters, mounting structures, AC/DC distribution boxes, and basic civil work).

## 4. Environmental Carbon Offset Modeling

### Standard Factor: Central Electricity Authority (CEA) of India
- **Publication:** *CO2 Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector (User Guide Version 19.0 / 2024)*
- **Grid Emissions Factor:** **$0.716\text{ kg CO}_2 / \text{kWh}$** ($716\text{ g CO}_2 / \text{kWh}$)
- **Methodology:**
  $$\text{Avoided CO}_2\text{ (kg/year)} = E_{\text{annual (kWh)}} \times 0.716\text{ kg CO}_2/\text{kWh}$$
- **Equivalency Benchmarks:**
  - $1\text{ Metric Ton CO}_2 \approx 45\text{ mature urban trees absorbing carbon over 10 years}$.

## 5. Solar Engineering Physical Deratings

| Parameter | Standard Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Specific Rooftop Requirement** | $8.0\text{ m}^2 / \text{kWp}$ | Standard for 540W–580W mono-PERC crystalline modules factoring inter-row pitch spacing for latitude 28°N. |
| **System Performance Ratio (PR)** | **$75.0\%$** ($0.75$) | Standard IEC 61724 baseline factoring inverter conversion efficiency ($98\%$), DC wiring ($1.5\%$), module mismatch ($1.5\%$), and tropical dust/soiling ($2\%$). |
| **Parapet & Obstacle Setback** | $70\%–80\%$ usable area | Factor accounts for rooftop parapets, water tanks, air conditioning outdoor units, and staircase headrooms. |
| **Shading Loss Profiling** | Low: $5\%$ \| Medium: $15\%$ \| High: $30\%$ | Discrete geometric horizon shading categories reflecting surrounding campus vegetation and higher academic wings. |
